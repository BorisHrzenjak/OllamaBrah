# OllamaBrah Design System Snapshot

## Product context

OllamaBrah presents itself as a serious local AI workbench for solo power users. The strongest existing brand voice appears on the marketing site, not in the desktop app.

## Current visual split

- Marketing site: authored, editorial, terminal-inflected, warm amber, serif + sans + mono typography
- Desktop app: generic dark SaaS / utility UI, Inter-based, theme-pack driven, visually competent but not distinctive

## Core tokens to preserve or reconcile

### Marketing direction

- Backgrounds: near-black with cool gray surfaces
- Accent: warm amber/orange
- Type: `DM Serif Display`, `IBM Plex Sans`, `JetBrains Mono`
- Mood: confident, tactile, slightly industrial

### Desktop app direction

- Backgrounds: flat charcoal stack
- Accent: amber by default, but fractured by many bundled theme palettes
- Type: `Inter`
- Mood: generic productivity tool

## Visual opportunities

1. Unify the app and marketing site under one authored visual language
2. Increase hierarchy and rhythm in dense modal and settings surfaces
3. Improve typographic craft, contrast discipline, and iconography consistency
